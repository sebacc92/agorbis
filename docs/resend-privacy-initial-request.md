# Consulta inicial a Resend

Preparada el 08-09-2026. Sin enviar por el agente; pendiente de envío por el
responsable. Copiar el cuerpo desde el correo asociado al equipo de Agorbis
en Resend. Esta primera consulta no requiere adjuntos.

- **Para:** `privacy@resend.com`
- **Asunto:** `Agorbis — Argentina data transfers and transactional email privacy`

```text
Hello Resend Privacy Team,

I am Sebastian Cardoso Castillo, responsible for Agorbis (agorbis.com), a
publicly accessible civic participation service based in Argentina. We are
reviewing our existing processing and a pending release.

Our documented Resend integration covers authentication and security emails.
We do not intend to include civic posts, political opinions, poll choices or
health-related submissions in email bodies. However, recipient addresses,
authentication messages and delivery metadata can reveal association with
the platform.

Please help us clarify:

1. Which international-transfer mechanism is available for Argentine personal
   data under Law 25,326 and AAIP rules? Can Plus Five Five, Inc. offer the
   official AAIP/RIPD Controller-to-Processor model clauses or an appropriate
   supplement? Please distinguish this from the EU/UK arrangements described
   in the standard DPA.
2. Which data is processed on our behalf, and which account, billing, usage or
   security data is processed for Resend's own purposes? Please identify the
   applicable importing entities.
3. Which countries and subprocessors apply to message content, addresses,
   delivery events, suppression lists, complaints, support records and backups?
4. What retention and deletion periods apply to those categories, distinguishing
   deletion of an Agorbis user, a message-removal request and termination of
   our Resend account?
5. Is the described authentication/security use permitted under Exhibit A's
   sensitive-data scope, including its contextual association with a civic
   service? Are specific controls or contractual changes required?

AAIP guidance and model clauses:
https://www.argentina.gob.ar/transferencias-internacionales

Please identify any plan requirement without making changes to our account.

Thank you,
Sebastian Cardoso Castillo
Agorbis — agorbis.com
```

El [DPA §7.3](https://resend.com/legal/dpa) publica el contacto de privacidad;
la [política de privacidad §19](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy) ofrece
`support@resend.com` como alternativa general para derivación. El
[pedido ampliado](TRANSFER_SAFEGUARDS_REQUESTS.md#resend) conserva más detalle
para seguimiento; no enviar ambos como casos separados.

## Documento de la cuenta

Entrar con el equipo de Agorbis a [Settings → Documents](https://resend.com/settings/documents)
y descargar su DPA. La [FAQ oficial](https://resend.com/security/gdpr) enlaza
esa pantalla e indica que el DPA se ejecuta con el alta y no necesita una
contrafirma individual. No hace falta completar esta descarga antes de enviar
la consulta. Falta recibir y revisar esa copia de cuenta; consultar la página
pública no acredita que ya se haya obtenido.

El plan, el dominio y su región de envío se revisarán con evidencia del panel;
la región de envío no demuestra por sí sola residencia de todos los datos.
Esta consulta no solicita API keys, mensajes ni datos de usuarios.
